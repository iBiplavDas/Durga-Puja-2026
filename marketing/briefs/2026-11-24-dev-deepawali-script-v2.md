## The narration script (for recording or Google AI Studio)

**Style:** *Adult male storyteller, deep warm baritone, slow and gripping, like telling a secret. Natural pauses at the commas and dots. A held breath before "शिव ने… एक ही बाण चलाया". Quiet awe on "हज़ार साल का अंधेरा"; a curious, inviting tone on the last question. Never theatrical.*

```
तीन असुरों को मारने के लिए… पूरा ब्रह्मांड हथियार बना।
ब्रह्मा का वरदान था: उनके नगर, बस एक बाण से टूटेंगे…
जब तीनों एक सीध में हों। हज़ार साल में, एक बार। तो वो बेखौफ़ हो गए।
तब शिव के लिए, सृष्टि खुद रथ बनी। धरती रथ, सूरज-चाँद पहिये, ब्रह्मा सारथी।
मेरु पर्वत बना धनुष, वासुकि नाग उसकी डोर… और स्वयं विष्णु, बाण बन गए।
फिर हज़ार साल बाद, वो पल आया। तीनों नगर, एक सीध में।
शिव ने… एक ही बाण चलाया।
और हज़ार साल का अंधेरा, एक पल में टूट गया।
उस रात, कार्तिक पूर्णिमा पर, देवताओं ने काशी में दीप जलाए।
इसीलिए आज भी, दिवाली के पंद्रह दिन बाद…
काशी, देवताओं की दिवाली मनाती है।
देव दीपावली।
पर उसी रात, देवता गंगा में क्यों उतरते हैं? वो… अगली कहानी।
```

132 words, about 75–80 s at storyteller pace. Record it as **one take with a pause between lines**. Leave 2 s of silence before the first line and after the last.

The ellipses (…) are pause cues for the voice. They never appear in the on-screen captions or the Instagram caption.

## Accuracy notes (for the caption and the comments)

- **Source:** Shiva Purana, Rudra Samhita, Yuddha Khanda.
- **Tripura was three brothers,** Tarakaksha, Kamalaksha and Vidyunmali, the sons of Tarakasura. Brahma refused them immortality. They asked instead for three cities (Maya built them in gold, silver and iron) that could fall only to a single arrow when all three lined up, once in 1,000 years. That condition is their "clever" mistake.
- **The cosmic chariot** (earth as the body, the sun and moon as wheels, Brahma as charioteer, Meru as the bow, Vasuki as the string, Vishnu as the arrow) follows the Shiva Purana. Some tellings add Agni at the arrow's tip, and in some Shiva burns Tripura with just a smile. Add this to the caption: *"Tellings differ by region; this follows the Shiva Purana."*
- **Kartik Purnima** is also called **Tripuri Purnima** for this victory. Dev Deepawali in Kashi falls on it, 15 days after Diwali.
- **Caption must-haves:** cite the Shiva Purana, and add "Visuals and narration made with AI." if the voice is AI-generated. If the owner records it, the line becomes "Visuals made with AI."
