# Hanuman, cinematic: Flow prompts for 7 shots

**The reel:** 30 seconds, 9:16, realistic and cinematic. Story: Hanuman, cursed as a child to forget his power, is reminded by Jambavan at the ocean and rises.

**How it comes together:** you generate the clips in Flow. The narration is recorded separately (see the end of this sheet) so one human-sounding voice runs through the whole reel. I do the edit: cuts timed to the voice, music, titles and the end card.

---

## Settings for every clip
- **Aspect:** 9:16 (portrait)
- **Model:** Omni Flash. If it's locked on the free tier, use Veo 3.1 Fast.
- **Length:** as listed per shot (4, 6 or 8 s)
- **Sound:** keep Flow's ambient sound. The prompts ask for **no speech, no music** so the clips cut together cleanly under the narration.
- If a clip comes out with text, extra limbs, a cartoon look or a melted face, regenerate it. Don't use it.

Add this **style line** to the end of every prompt:
> Photorealistic live-action epic film, anamorphic lens, shallow depth of field, film grain, deep teal shadows and molten gold highlights, volumetric light. No text, no subtitles, no watermark, no music, no speech.

---

## Step 0: the two character references (do these first)
Create these as **images** in Flow, or free in the Gemini app ("Create image"). Then add them to Flow as **Ingredients**. They keep Hanuman and Jambavan identical in every shot.

**Hanuman reference**
> Photorealistic cinematic full-body portrait of Hanuman as an epic mythological warrior: tall powerful muscular build, a noble monkey face with wise intense amber eyes, reddish-golden fur, an ornate golden crown, gold armbands and a gold necklace, a saffron-red dhoti with a gold border, a heavy golden mace resting on his right shoulder, a long tail curving behind him. Standing on dark wet coastal rocks at dusk, whole figure and feet in frame, soft overcast light, live-action film-still realism, not cartoon, no text.

**Jambavan reference**
> Photorealistic cinematic full-body portrait of Jambavan, the ancient wise bear king from the Ramayana: a huge upright grey-brown bear-like elder warrior with a long white mane and beard, kind deep eyes, a simple dark ochre shawl and rudraksha bead necklaces, holding a tall wooden staff, standing on dark coastal rocks at dusk, whole figure in frame, soft overcast light, live-action film-still realism, not cartoon, no text.

## Day 1 (about 34 credits): the four shots that make or break it

### Shot 1: the hook (4 s, Ingredient: Hanuman)
> Medium shot, low angle. Hanuman sits motionless on a black rock on a wild stormy seashore at dusk, head bowed in defeat, his golden mace resting against his shoulder, wind tugging at his saffron cloth, a huge wave breaking white behind him. At the end he lifts his eyes slightly, not his head. Slow push in towards his face. Cold blue storm light, faint golden rim light from the horizon, sea spray in the air. Sound: strong wind and crashing waves.

### Shot 4: the impossible ocean (6 s, Ingredient: Hanuman)
> Wide aerial shot over a rocky shoreline at dusk: a great army of vanara warriors stands silent at the water's edge facing an endless storming ocean; Hanuman sits apart on a rock in the lower right with his head bowed. Huge waves roll in and break across the rocks; nobody steps forward. A faint golden glow of a distant island on the horizon. Slow drone push out over the sea towards the horizon. Storm-blue light, shafts of grey light through heavy clouds. Sound: roaring sea, wind, distant thunder.

### Shot 5: Jambavan's reminder (6 s, Ingredients: Hanuman + Jambavan)
> Close two-shot at eye level on the stormy seashore: Jambavan, the old bear king, at frame left places his large paw firmly on Hanuman's left shoulder and leans in, speaking with quiet conviction; Hanuman at frame right slowly raises his head and meets his eyes. Both heads fully in frame. Slow arc from Jambavan's side to Hanuman's face. Cold blue light, with the first warm golden sunlight breaking through the clouds onto Hanuman's face. Sound: wind and waves, no speech.

### Shot 6: the awakening (4 s, Ingredient: Hanuman)
> Extreme close-up on Hanuman's amber eyes as they flare with golden light; a shockwave of wind blows spray and sand outward around him and he rises to his full height gripping his golden mace, filmed in a low-angle tilt up. Warm golden light blazing from behind him through parting storm clouds, glowing embers in the air. Fast push in on the eyes, then tilt up as he rises. Sound: deep rumble and a rush of wind.

## Day 2 (about 27 credits): the flashbacks and the leap

### Shot 2: the sun (6 s, no Ingredient)
> Wide shot of a vast golden sunrise sky over misty forested hills: a small child vanara, seen only as a dark backlit silhouette with a curling tail, springs from a hilltop and flies upward towards a huge blazing sun, one arm stretched out, until the sun's glare fills the top of the frame. Camera tilts up following the leap. Blinding golden backlight, lens flare, heat shimmer. Sound: a warm whoosh of air.

### Shot 3: the curse (6 s, no Ingredient)
> Medium wide shot inside a forest hermitage at night: two elderly sages with long white beards in simple saffron robes stand beside a small sacred fire; one raises an open palm. A small child vanara sits in the foreground with his back to camera. The fire flares and then dims, and a soft golden glow around the child fades into darkness. Locked-off camera. Warm firelight against deep blue night. Sound: crackling fire, a low hush.

### Shot 7: the leap (4 s, Ingredient: Hanuman)
> Wide low-angle shot from the shore rocks: Hanuman leaps from the rock and soars out over the stormy ocean towards a glowing island on the horizon, golden mace raised, small but heroic against vast parting clouds, flying into golden light. Camera tilts up and holds as he flies away. Golden sunlight bursting through the clouds, silver spray below. Sound: a powerful gust of wind and spray.

## The narration (one voice for the whole reel)

Choose one of these:
- **Record it yourself** on your phone, in a quiet room, about 30 cm from the mic. A real voice is the most "human" there is.
- **Free AI voice:** Google AI Studio (aistudio.google.com) → *Generate speech*. Pick a deep male voice and paste the style line and script below.

**Style:** *Adult male narrator, deep warm baritone, slow and sincere, natural pauses, building in intensity on the last line, never theatrical.*

| Time | Line |
|---|---|
| 0–4 s | उसे श्राप मिला था… अपनी ही ताकत भूल जाने का। |
| 4–9 s | बचपन में, उसने सूरज को निगलना चाहा था। |
| 9–14 s | ऋषियों ने कहा: तुम अपनी शक्ति भूल जाओगे। |
| 14–19 s | सालों बाद, सामने था अथाह समुद्र। सब हार चुके थे। |
| 19–23 s | तब जाम्बवान बोले: तुम पवनपुत्र हो। |
| 23–27 s | और उसे सब याद आ गया। |
| 27–30 s | *(silence: the leap)* |
