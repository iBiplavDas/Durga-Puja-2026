## Why it works
1. **The hook is an emotional paradox, not a fact:** an enemy who loved God. The question it raises lasts the whole reel.
2. **It's a complete story arc in about 100 s:** setup, mistake, consequence, choice, payoff, cliffhanger. Every 10–15 s something new happens, so retention holds even at nearly two minutes.
3. **Captions are synced word by word,** 2–4 words at a time and changing every second. They're cream small-caps serif with one key word in teal, always in the same spot. The reel works with sound off, and the eye never has to hunt for the text.
4. **It has one ownable visual world:**
   - an indigo starry sky with a crescent moon always at top right;
   - radiating light rays;
   - patterned clouds along the bottom;
   - a golden Vaikuntha gate as the recurring set;
   - stage touches (stars hanging on strings, a red curtain).

   It reads as a *puppet theatre of the gods*.
5. **The animation is simple, and the art direction carries it.** Flat cut-out characters with jointed limbs slide, raise arms and turn. Push-ins give close-ups. Light changes the mood:
   - white bloom means divine;
   - orange-red means curse or anger;
   - a dark, empty sky means separation.

   Sets are reused, so it's cheap to make at scale.
6. **Abstract ideas get visual metaphors:** 7 gold stars stand for seven lifetimes, and 3 red stars for three lives as enemies.
7. **It's a series by design:** the cliffhanger plus "Follow, that is the next story" in the video *and* the caption. That turns views into follows.
8. **It's brand-safe devotion:** love, choice and grace. No fear, no threats.

## What this means for us
- **Story depth beats realism.** Our recent reels are 20–60 s mood pieces. This one is a gripping plot.
- **Captions:** every reel gets word-synced captions in a fixed spot, with one highlighted word.
- **A recurring world:** fixed sets, a fixed palette and fixed motifs, so viewers recognise the page in half a second.
- **Hooks:** an emotional paradox or a "why" question.
- **Endings:** a Part 2 cliffhanger, every time.
- **Our own look, not theirs:** for example Indian folk-art cut-outs (Pattachitra or shadow-puppet inspired), warm temple palette.
