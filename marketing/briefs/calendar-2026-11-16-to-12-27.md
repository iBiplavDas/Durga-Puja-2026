## What changed from the Growth Plan, and why

| Change | Evidence |
|---|---|
| **Three tiers instead of three equal story reels a week:** one big cinematic story (60–90 s), one "Why" or festival reel (35–50 s), one verse reel (20–30 s). | Production. A Flow story takes about 12 clips of the owner's time and credits, so three a week isn't realistic. The Why and verse reels can be made in-house: Canva storybook frames, the animation kit and an AI voice. This also runs the "cinematic vs illustrated" style test for free. |
| **Big stories run 60–90 s**, not 35–50 s. | The Jaya-Vijaya teardown: a full arc at 1:48 got 700K likes. Why and verse reels stay short. |
| **"One verse" becomes a 20–30 s reel** ("कृष्ण कहते हैं" / "Krishna says"), not a carousel. | Audit: photos and carousel conversions got 0–4 likes. Swipe: "God speaks to you" reels ran 6–43× their channel's median. Ours uses **real verses with chapter and verse cited**. |
| **Festival stories go out 2–4 days before the festival.** | Audit: route maps posted before the nights out got 238 and 157 likes; the same content on the night got 0–1. Swipe: festival-timed kathas were the top pattern. |
| **Every big story's Part 2 follows within a week.** | The cliffhanger only turns views into follows if Part 2 arrives while people remember. |
| **One reel a day at most**, including the daily posting session's reels. | Audit: days with 13 posts had a median of 4 likes. |

## Every reel, every time

- **Hook:** a paradox, a "why", or a famous-figure question. Up on screen from frame 1 and spoken by 3 s.
- **Captions:** synced word by word, 2–4 words at a time, in one fixed spot inside the safe zone, with one gold word.
- **Ask:** one per reel, a send, a save or a follow. For comments, end the caption on an easy question.
- **Caption:** cite the source, disclose AI ("Visuals and narration made with AI."), credit any CC media. Built with `ig-caption`.
- **Pinned comment:** the source with chapter, plus "where you live, how is this story told?"
- **First-hour replies:** use `ig-reply`.

## Stories (daily, 3–5 frames)

- **Day after a reel:** a quiz sticker from that reel ("कितने दिन भीष्म…?", "how many days did Bhishma…?").
- **The day before a festival reel:** a poll ("आप देव दीपावली मनाते हैं?", "do you celebrate Dev Deepawali?").
- **Thursdays:** a question box ("कौन-सी कहानी अगली?", "which story next?"). Answers feed next month's calendar.
