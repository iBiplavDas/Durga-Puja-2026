*Built with the `ig-audit` skill from the Monday stats snapshot (`engage-data` branch, `stats/2026-11-14.json`): 50 posts, 18 Oct to 14 Nov.*

## First, the data problem

**The stats only have likes and comments.** The insights call fails with `(#10) Application does not have permission for this action`. Without views, reach, non-follower share, the 3-second hold, shares and follows, the skill's main measures can't be worked out. Everything below ranks on **likes ÷ the page's median likes per reel (9)**. That's a rough stand-in.

**The fix:** the `IG_ACCESS_TOKEN` repo secret needs the `instagram_manage_insights` permission. The owner regenerates the token with that permission in the Meta developer app and updates the secret. After that, Monday's `engage.yml` stats run records views, reach, saves and shares on its own. Until then, a screenshot of Insights for each story reel (views, accounts reached, % non-followers, shares, average watch time, and the retention graph) is the best input.

## What the data says

1. **Content timed to a need wins, and the same content a day late dies** *(strong)*. Route maps posted before the nights people went out (18–19 Oct) got 238 and 157 likes. Route maps posted on Navami night (20 Oct), when people were already out, got 0 and 1. This is the same pattern as the swipe file's top finding: kathas timed to the week's festival. **For Phase 2:** post festival stories 2–4 days *before* the festival, not on the day.
2. **Too many posts in a day dilutes all of them** *(moderate)*. The 13 posts on 20 Oct had a median of 4 likes, and the 8 on 21 Oct a median of 4. Days with 1–3 posts (26 Oct, 31 Oct, 2 Nov) had medians of 27–98. Keep the cadence at **one story reel a day at most**. The Tue/Thu/Sun plan already does this.
3. **Number-and-place hooks fill the top** *(moderate)*. 6 of the top 7 have a number ("15 pandals", "48 minutes, 108 lamps", "12 days earlier", "four days"). The flops with numbers ("Navratri Day 8") use a number as a label, not a stake. That supports the v2 Dev Deepawali hook ("तीन असुर… हज़ार साल").
4. **Photos and carousel conversions don't travel** *(strong, n=7, every one at 0–4 likes)*.
5. **Comments only come from questions that are easy to answer** *(weak)*. 22 of the page's 31 comments landed on one route reel. "How many pandals did you see this year?" drew 4. Story reels should end on an easy question, like "आपने ये कहानी पहले सुनी थी?" ("had you heard this story before?"), as well as the Part 2 cliffhanger.

Day of week and time show nothing beyond the volume effect. Not worth optimising.

---

**STOP:** photo posts; converting carousels into reels; posting festival content on the day itself; more than one reel a day.

**DO MORE:** stories timed 2–4 days ahead of the festival; hooks with a number at stake; one easy question at the end of the caption.

**FIX FIRST:** the insights permission on the posting token. Until then, Phase 2 can't be measured, only guessed at.

These go to `/ig-plan` for the next calendar update, alongside `swipe-2026-11.md`.

## Update, 15 Nov 13:10 IST: insights now come through

After the owner saved a new token, a manual stats run (`stats/2026-11-15.json` on `engage-data`) returned views, reach, saves and shares for all 50 posts.

- **Page baseline:** median reel views **270**, median reach **235**.
- **Biggest hit:** the Lake Market & Kalighat route got **20,353 views**, 75× the median, with **238 shares and 256 saves**. That confirms finding 1: content timed to a need gets sent on.

**The story reels:**

| Reel | Age | Views | Reach | Shares | Saves | × median views |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ganesha and Kartikeya race | 14 days | 165 | 157 | 0 | 0 | 0.6× |
| Chhath | 18 hours | 125 | 110 | 0 | 0 | 0.5× |
| Hanuman | 1 hour | 69 | 51 | 0 | 0 | too early |
| Jagaddhatri | 1 hour | 114 | 98 | 0 | 0 | too early |

**What this means:**
- After 14 days the Ganesha reel had reached 157 accounts, with no shares and no saves. Instagram tested it on a small group and stopped.
- ~~The page's existing followers came for Kolkata routes and are a mismatch for Hindi stories.~~ **Corrected the same day by the follower split** (below): almost all reach already comes from non-followers, so the follower base isn't the bottleneck.
- **The bar for the Dev Deepawali v2 reel:** shares above 0 and reach above the 235 median within 48 hours.
- **Follower vs non-follower split (added to `engage.py stats` on 15 Nov):** **96% of reach in the last 7 days and 99.6% in the last 28 days came from non-followers** (841 of 875; 40,479 of 40,630). Followers barely see the posts at all.
  - So Instagram *is* testing the story reels on new people. Those people aren't staying to watch or sending the reels on, which is a hook and story problem, not an audience problem. The Jaya-Vijaya rules and the v2 script are aimed at exactly that.
  - Instagram doesn't split a single reel by follower type ("Incompatible breakdowns (follow_type) for metric (reach)"). The day-by-day account split is the stand-in: 14 Nov (the Chhath reel) reached 750 non-followers, against 6–33 a day on days with no post.
