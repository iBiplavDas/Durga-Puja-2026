# Swipe file: Hindi mythology and spiritual Shorts (Nov 2026)

*Built 15 Nov 2026 with the `ig-viral` skill. `/ig-reel` and `/ig-plan` read the copy at `~/.claude/instagram/swipe.md`; this repo copy is the master. Run it again monthly.*

**Sample:**
- 436 Shorts from 12 YouTube channels, each channel's last ~40.
- The channels: 5 direct (mythology stories), 4 adjacent (vrat katha, "God speaks" guidance) and 3 outsized (format only).
- Each Short is ranked by **views ÷ that channel's own median**. Above 3× is a signal; below 1.5× is a normal day.

**Limits, stated plainly:**
1. **YouTube, not Instagram.** Instagram needs a login, and this skill never logs in on your behalf. The hook grammar carries over, but treat these as leads, not proof.
2. **Titles, not spoken hooks.** YouTube rate-limited the caption download, and I didn't try to get around it. On Shorts the title is usually the on-screen hook, but not always.
3. **The skill's formula classifier only reads English,** so 435 of 436 rows came out "unclassified". The patterns below come from reading the outliers by hand.

**Confidence:**
- **Moderate** for patterns 1 and 2, seen across several accounts.
- **Low to moderate** for patterns 3 and 4, each mostly one account.

## What's over-indexing

**1. A katha timed to what people are doing this week** *(4 accounts, 9 of the top 24)*
- Nirjala Ekadashi story: 40.9×, 7.4×, 5.7×. Gangaur: 11.2×. Sita's first Chhath: 11.5×. "Why barley in Navratri?": 4.5×.
- Across all 436, festival tie-ins make up 15% of each channel's top third against 9% of its bottom third.
- **For us:** the festival dates are the calendar. Every festival on the calendar gets a *story*, not a greeting card.

**2. A "who / why / how old" question about a famous figure, with a surprising answer** *(2 accounts, 6 outliers)*
- "Who were Mandodari's two husbands?" 13×. "How old was Krishna when he died?" 8.1×. "How old was Krishna in Dwapar Yuga?" 6.6×. "Why no rakhi in Bhadra?" 2.7×.
- **Short, a single question, and a name everyone knows.** That's the trivia version of our "why" hook.

**3. A paradox or symmetry line about a famous text** *(1 outsized account, 4 outliers, 0.8–4.4M views)*
- "The Mahabharata begins with a dog. It ends with one too." 27.7×.
- "Indra waited 101 years to know the Self." 11.3×.
- "Narada knew many sciences. He was still miserable." 5.2×.
- **This independently backs the Jaya-Vijaya paradox rule:** a number, a god, and a twist that pays off spiritually.

**4. God speaking to you directly ("कृष्ण वाणी / शिव वाणी")** *(1 small adjacent account, 6k subscribers, 8 of its top reels)*
- "जब भी तुम्हें सहायता की आवश्यकता हो" ("whenever you need help"): 42.7×.
- "जब भी कोई ईश्वर का नाम लेता है" ("whenever anyone takes God's name"): 14.2×.
- These are second-person, hope-and-guidance lines. That fits the page's purpose exactly.
- **The risk:** that account makes up its "vani" lines. Our version must use **real verses with chapter and verse cited** (Gita, Upanishads). That accuracy is the moat.

**Structural differences:**
- The top third and bottom third hooks are about the same length (median 9 words vs 8). Length isn't what separates them.
- Bait emoji (😱🔥) barely move the needle (8% of the top third vs 6% of the bottom). Keep them out, as `voice-stories.md` already says.

**Unclassified:**
- All the Hindi rows. The `hooks.json` formulas are built for business and creator content. Patterns 1, 2 and 4 aren't in it at all.
- **Worth adding as page-specific formulas:** "Festival Why", "Famous-Figure Question" and "God Speaks to You".
