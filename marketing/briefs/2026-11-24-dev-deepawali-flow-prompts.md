# Dev Deepawali, the story version: Flow prompts for 12 shots

**The reel:** about 70 seconds, 9:16, realistic and cinematic. Posts **Tue 24 Nov, 7:27 pm IST**.

**The story** (Shiva Purana, Rudra Samhita):
- Brahma's boon: Tripurasura's three flying cities can be destroyed only by **one arrow**, at the **one moment in 1,000 years** when all three line up.
- The gods build Shiva a **chariot out of the cosmos**:
  - The earth is the chariot, and the sun and moon are its wheels.
  - Brahma drives it.
  - Mount Meru is the bow, and the serpent Vasuki is its string.
  - Vishnu himself becomes the arrow.
- The cities align. One arrow, and the darkness ends.
- The gods light lamps in Kashi. That is Dev Deepawali.
- **Cliffhanger for Part 2:** why do the gods come down to bathe in the Ganga that same night?

## Day 1: the cosmic weapon (the shots that make this reel)

### Shot 4: the chariot of the cosmos (8 s) · NEW
> Epic wide shot in deep space above the curve of a glowing Earth: a colossal golden divine chariot forms out of light, its body shaped from the Earth's own land and oceans, its two giant wheels a blazing golden sun on one side and a glowing silver full moon on the other, both slowly turning. A radiant four-faced elder god with a white beard in golden robes stands at the front holding the reins of light. Slow orbiting camera move around the chariot as it completes itself. Stars, nebula clouds and shafts of golden light. Sound: deep cosmic hum, a rising rush like wind.

### Shot 5: the bow, the string, the arrow (8 s) · NEW
> Epic wide shot in a cosmic night sky: an entire snow-capped golden mountain bends into the shape of a gigantic bow; a vast glowing serpent with shimmering silver-blue scales stretches tight from tip to tip as its bowstring; along the bow a single colossal arrow forms out of brilliant blue-white light, its tip blazing with fire. Slow push in along the length of the bow towards the glowing arrow. Stars, nebula haze, golden and blue light. Sound: a deep creak of stone, the hiss of the serpent string tightening, a low choir-like hum (no words).

### Shot 7: the arrow (6 s) · same as old shot 3
> Wide low-angle shot from behind and below a colossal dark silhouette of Lord Shiva standing on a Himalayan summit at night, long matted hair flowing in the wind, a small crescent moon glinting in his hair, two arms drawing a gigantic bow aimed high into a stormy sky where three huge glowing fortress-cities hang in a single straight line. He draws the bow to full stretch, the arrowhead ignites with blinding golden fire, and he releases it; the arrow streaks upward towards the cities. Slow push in behind him, then a fast tilt up following the arrow. Pure backlit silhouette against pale storm clouds, never showing his face. Sound: the deep creak of the bow, then a roaring whoosh.

### Shot 1: the hook (4 s) · same as old shot 1

She gently sets the diya onto the water and lets go; it drifts slowly towards the lens as its golden reflection trembles. Very slow push in at water level. Only the warm flame lights the hands and the water. Sound: soft water lapping, a single distant temple bell.

## Day 2: the boon, the cities, the moment

### Shot 2: Brahma's boon (6 s) · NEW
> Medium wide shot in a vast golden cosmic sky: a radiant four-faced elder god with a long white beard, in golden robes, seated on a giant glowing pink lotus floating among clouds, slowly raises one open palm in blessing. Above him, three ghostly outlines of colossal fortress-cities, one gold, one silver, one dark iron, shimmer into existence in the sky. Slow push in and tilt up from the god to the forming cities. Warm golden light, soft haze, drifting lotus petals. Sound: a soft resonant bell tone, a low hum.

### Shot 3: Tripura, the three flying cities (6 s) · same as old shot 2
> Wide low-angle shot of a vast stormy night sky above dark snow-capped Himalayan peaks: three colossal ancient fortress-cities float in the sky, one built of gold, one of silver, one of dark iron, each glowing red-hot at its core. They drift slowly across the sky as lightning flickers around them and burning embers fall onto the peaks below. Slow tilt up from the peaks to the cities. Storm-blue night lit by red fire and white lightning. Sound: distant thunder, a low rumbling wind.

### Shot 6: the moment, after 1,000 years (6 s) · NEW
> Wide shot of a stormy cosmic night sky: three colossal glowing fortress-cities, one gold, one silver, one dark iron, drift slowly from different directions until they line up perfectly one behind the other in a single straight row; at the instant they align, a thin beam of white light passes through all three and the clouds around them go still. Locked-off camera, then a very slow push in. Storm-blue sky, red glow of the cities, a silver full moon behind them. Sound: wind dying away into tense silence, a single low drumbeat.

### Shot 12: the cliffhanger (6 s) · NEW
> Wide shot from the top of the stone ghat steps of Varanasi at night looking down at the Ganga: countless small diyas on the steps and floating on the river under a bright full moon; from the moonlit sky, several tall translucent figures made of soft golden light slowly descend like drifting lanterns towards the river and touch the water, sending gentle golden ripples among the floating lamps. Slow push out and down the steps. Gold light, deep indigo sky, soft mist on the water. Sound: river water, distant bells, a soft shimmering hum.

## Day 3: Kashi tonight

### Shot 8: darkness breaks (4 s) · same as old shot 4
> Wide shot of a stormy night sky: three huge glowing fortress-cities hang in one straight line as a blazing golden arrow pierces through all three. They burst silently into a vast slow shower of golden sparks that drift down through the clouds like falling lamps, and the storm clouds part to reveal a bright full moon. Slow tilt down following the falling sparks. Sound: a deep boom fading into a soft shimmering hush.

### Shot 9: the ghats come alive (6 s) · same as old shot 5
> Wide shot from a wooden boat drifting on the Ganga at night, looking at the ghats of Varanasi: endless worn stone steps rising from the river covered in rows of countless small clay diyas, old temples and palaces above, marigold garlands, incense smoke, a bright full moon high on the right. Tiny golden sparks drift down from the sky and settle on the steps as more rows of diyas flicker to life; other wooden boats glide past and the long gold reflections shimmer on the dark river. Slow lateral glide along the ghats. Sound: river water, oars, distant temple bells and soft chanting crowd murmur.

### Shot 10: the Ganga aarti (4 s) · same as old shot 6
> Medium shot at the river's edge at night in Varanasi: a row of young priests in saffron and white dhotis raise tall multi-tiered brass lamps blazing with fire, faces in soft profile, swinging the lamps in slow synchronised circles; flames trail through the air, thick incense smoke curls up, marigold petals fall. Slow low-angle arc around the lamps. Strong warm firelight on faces and brass against a dark indigo river and sky. Sound: conch shells, ringing bells, crackling flame.

### Shot 11: the river of light (4 s) · same as old shot 7
> High aerial wide shot over the Ganga at night: the whole crescent-shaped riverfront of Varanasi glows gold with countless diyas along every ghat, hundreds of small floating lamps drift on the dark river, a bright full moon in a deep indigo sky. Slow aerial pull back and rise, then hold in stillness. Sound: a soft wind and very distant bells.
