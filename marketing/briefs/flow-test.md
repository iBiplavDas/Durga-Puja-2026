**Goal:** before paying for Google AI Pro, check that Flow can animate our characters well:
- faces stay the same as our frames
- the gods look correct

**Cost:** nothing. Every Google account gets **50 free Flow credits a day**. The four clips below use about 48. If something fails, finish tomorrow with the new day's credits.

## Setup (2 minutes)
1. Open **labs.google/flow** on a laptop and sign in with the Google account you'd later subscribe on.
2. Start a new project. In settings choose **9:16 (portrait)**, **8 seconds**, model **Omni Flash** (about 12 credits a clip).
   - If Omni Flash is locked on the free tier, use **Veo 3.1 Fast** (about 20 credits a clip). That gives two clips today and two tomorrow.

3. For each clip, use **Frames to video** (or "start frame"). Upload the start frame named below and paste the prompt exactly.
4. Turn sound **off** if there is a switch. Our Hindi narration goes on top.

## The four clips

Each clip tests something different. The first three are animating a single image; the last is the hardest test.

### Clip 1: the flight
**Tests:** big motion, wings, speed.

Start frame: `1-kartikeya-flight.jpg`
> The boy Kartikeya rides his flying blue peacock fast over the snowy Himalayas. The peacock beats its wings strongly, its long tail feathers stream behind, Kartikeya's yellow scarf flutters in the wind and he smiles with joy, holding his golden spear steady. Clouds rush past below. The camera tracks alongside them. Pixar-style 3D animation, golden sunlight, smooth motion.

### Clip 2: the acting
**Tests:** expression, eyes and hands. This is the "emotional" moment.

Start frame: `2-ganesha-mouse.jpg`
> Little Ganesha sits on the snowy rock and looks down thoughtfully at his tiny mouse, who looks back up at him. Then Ganesha slowly raises his head, his eyes light up with an idea, he smiles warmly and folds his small hands together. The mouse tilts its head. Gentle snow drifts. Pixar-style 3D animation, warm golden light, slow and tender.

### Clip 3: the talking parents
**Tests:** faces and gestures of two adults, and whether a mouth moves naturally.

Start frame: `3-shiva-parvati.jpg`
> Lord Shiva smiles and speaks, raising his finger as he announces a challenge, his eyes kind. Parvati beside him looks at him and smiles, holding out the glowing golden mango in her palm; it pulses softly with light. The snake around Shiva's neck sways gently. Snowy Kailash behind at sunset. Pixar-style 3D animation, calm and warm.

### Clip 4: the circling, the hardest one
**Tests:** keeping Ganesha consistent while he moves around other characters. This is the shot we couldn't fake in the storybook version.

Use **Ingredients** (or "add reference images") instead of a start frame. Add two images: `2-ganesha-mouse.jpg` (for Ganesha) and `3-shiva-parvati.jpg` (for Shiva and Parvati).
> Shiva and Parvati sit together on snowy Mount Kailash, smiling. Little Ganesha, the same character as in the reference, walks slowly all the way around them with his hands folded in prayer, looking up at them lovingly; a soft golden trail of light follows his footsteps in the snow. Wide shot, camera gently circling with him. Pixar-style 3D animation, golden hour.

If clip 4 doesn't keep Ganesha's face, try once more with only `4-ganesha-kartikeya-mango.jpg` as the start frame:
> …the little elephant-headed Ganesha walks out of frame and around…

## What to check in each clip
- [ ] **Faces** look like our start frames, not a different character.
- [ ] **Ganesha:** elephant head, one tusk, four small arms, crown. No extra or missing arms, and a trunk that doesn't melt.
- [ ] **Kartikeya:** one spear, and his peacock stays a peacock.
- [ ] **Shiva:** crescent moon, snake and matted hair stay put. **Parvati:** crown and red saree.
- [ ] **Hands and feet** don't morph. Nothing flickers or warps at the edges.
- [ ] **Motion** looks deliberate, not floaty or rubbery.
- [ ] Would you stop scrolling for it?
