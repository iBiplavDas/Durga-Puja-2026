# voice-stories.md · Phase 2 story page (draft, 15 Nov 2026)

The voice for the pan-India Hindu story reels. `marketing/voice.md` stays as it is for the Bengali pujo posts the daily posting session still makes. **The owner should review this draft.** Once approved, either point the ig-* skills here or merge it into `voice.md`.

## Who we are
- **Name:** to be decided (the owner rejected "Ko Jagarti"). The handle is the current Instagram page; the end card shows the handle.
- **What we do, in one sentence:** gripping, little-known stories of Hindu gods, told cinematically in Hindi, each leaving you with something to live by.
- **Who we're talking to:** Hindi-speaking teens and adults, 16 to 40, across India, scrolling Reels in the evening. They grew up with these stories but never heard the twist. **Not children.**
- **What we sell:** nothing for now. Monetisation (labelled brand deals) comes later, only with the owner's approval.

## What we sound like
- **Narrator:** an adult male storyteller, 35 to 45, with a deep warm baritone. Slow and gripping, as if telling you a secret, with natural pauses and a held breath before the turn. Never theatrical, never preachy. The best option is a real recording; next best is Google AI Studio speech. No robotic TTS.
- **Language:**
  - Narration and on-screen captions: Hindi in Devanagari.
  - Caption body: English, for Instagram search.
  - The caption opens with a Hindi hook line, and the question at the end can be in Hindi.
- **Every reel's structure** (from the Jaya-Vijaya teardown in `marketing/briefs/`):
  1. **Hook in the first 3 s:** a paradox or a "why" ("एक ऐसा असुर, जिसे मारने के लिए पूरे ब्रह्मांड को हथियार बनना पड़ा").
  2. **A full arc in 45–110 s:** setup, mistake or problem, consequence, choice or turn, payoff. Something new every 10–15 s.
  3. **The lesson,** shown and not lectured: one line at most.
  4. **A cliffhanger:** "…वो अगली कहानी।" Part 2 is set up in both the video and the caption.
- **On screen:** Hindi captions appear word by word, 2–4 words at a time, always in the same spot, with one key word in gold. No other lettering is generated inside the clips.
- **Look:** cinematic and photorealistic: anamorphic lens, indigo night against molten gold firelight, smoke, reflections. Respectful deities; for fierce or battle forms, use a silhouette. An illustrated "story world" is a candidate second format; the owner hasn't decided yet.
- **Words we like:** कथा, रहस्य, वरदान, श्राप, भक्ति, लीला, "क्या आप जानते हैं", plus Purana and place names.
- **Words we never use:** "viral", "must watch", "you won't believe", "stop scrolling", "share or bad luck", "type Jai if you're a true devotee", "only 1% know".
- **Emoji in captions:** two or three as punctuation (🔱 🪔 🙏), never as the first character.

## Caption layout
1. A Hindi hook line that survives the "… more" cut.
2. 3–5 short English lines of story context.
3. One ask: send it to someone, or a curiosity question for comments.
4. The source: Purana, Kanda or Parva.
5. Search keywords in brackets.
6. "Visuals and narration made with AI." when true, plus music and photo credits.
7. Three hashtags.

## Our positions
1. These stories are about love, choice and grace, never fear.
2. We cite the scripture, and where regions tell it differently, we say so.
3. No politics, and no putting down any religion or community.
4. Every paid post is labelled Sponsored. A sponsor never changes a story.
